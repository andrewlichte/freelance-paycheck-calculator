What to put on a freelance invoice

  • Your details and the client's: names, business names, addresses and an email for questions.
  • A unique invoice number, so you and the client can both refer to it. Number them in order: INV-001, INV-002 and so on.
  • Invoice date and due date. "Net 14" or "Net 30" is common; shorter terms get you paid sooner.
  • What you did: one line per deliverable or block of hours, with quantity, rate and amount.
  • Totals, including any tax or discount, and the currency.
  • How to pay you: bank details, PayPal, Stripe link or whatever you accept.

How to get invoices paid on time

Send the invoice the day the work is done, make paying you as easy as one click, state the due date clearly, and follow up the day after it passes. Mention a late fee in your terms up front if you plan to charge one; the late payment fee calculator shows what to charge.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Click Print or save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF (or Microsoft Print to PDF) as the printer. Only the invoice is printed, not the form or the rest of the page.

What payment terms should a freelancer use?

Net 14 or Net 30 are the most common. Shorter terms, or asking for a deposit up front on larger jobs, help with cash flow. Whatever you choose, put the actual due date on the invoice, not just the terms.
